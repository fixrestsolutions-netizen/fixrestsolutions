Fixrest is an institutional-grade acquisition firm. We engineer liquidity for complex real estate portfolios and secure hyper-scale data center infrastructure across emerging digital frontiers.

Strategic Origination

Operating exclusively off-market, we source high-yield Single Family Rental (SFR) assets across the United States. We serve as a direct, frictionless pipeline for Private Equity firms looking to deploy capital intensely without public market exposure.

Southern African Operations

From our headquarters in Durban and Johannesburg, we identify and vet institutional-grade land for AI data centers. We leverage local economic dynamics to provide our partners with unmatched Capex efficiency in the SADC region.

Division I: Real Estate Liquidity

Fiduciary Solutions
& Portfolio Exits.

We specialize in the rapid valuation, acquisition, and transition of inherited, probate, and non-performing banking assets.

Deceased Estates

We partner with legal executors to liquidate inherited properties swiftly. By acquiring properties "as-is," families bypass the holding costs and emotional friction of traditional market listings, ensuring a dignified transfer of capital.

Banking Partnerships

For financial institutions holding non-performing real estate (REO), time equates to lost yield. Fixrest acquires distressed portfolios directly, providing immediate balance-sheet relief.

Absolute Discretion

Maintaining the integrity of the asset's history is paramount. Our acquisitions are off-market, ensuring strict privacy for the sellers and a sophisticated transition of title.

Division II: Infrastructure

Hyper-Scale AI
Site Selection.

Acting as the localized vanguard for international Private Equity, we secure the physical backbone of the 2026 artificial intelligence boom in Southern Africa.

The African Advantage

The global demand for GPU clusters requires vast power and space. Southern Africa provides a highly favorable ZAR/USD exchange rate, making land acquisition and localized construction exceptionally cost-effective for US and European funds.

The Technical Guarantee

We do not just find land; we deliver a de-risked asset. Our selection criteria mandate a minimum 10MW grid capacity, specialized industrial zoning, and direct proximity to critical subsea fiber cables like 2Africa and Equiano.
