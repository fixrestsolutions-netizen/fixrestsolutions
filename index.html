Division I: Asset Liquidity

Fiduciary Solutions

For executors and legal counsel managing deceased estates, Fixrest provides a streamlined, capital-backed exit strategy. We specialize in the immediate valuation and acquisition of complex probate properties, mitigating the long-term holding costs and administrative burdens typically associated with estate liquidation.

Banking & REO

We partner with financial institutions to manage non-performing assets. Our approach focuses on clean, rapid balance-sheet relief. By acquiring assets in their current condition, we eliminate the need for bank-led refurbishments or protracted marketing periods, ensuring a swift recovery of capital.

Discreet Transactions

Maintaining the integrity of the asset's history is paramount. Our acquisition process is conducted off-market, ensuring privacy for the sellers and a sophisticated transition of title that respects the sensitivity of high-value portfolios.

Division II: Infrastructure

AI Data Centre Site Selection

Southern Africa is positioned as the primary gateway for the continent’s digital expansion. Fixrest identifies and secures high-tier industrial land suitable for hyper-scale data centers. Our selection criteria focus on three non-negotiable pillars: Grid Capacity (10MW+ accessibility), Proximity to Fiber Landing Stations, and Regulatory Zoning Compliance.

For Private Equity firms, we act as the localized "boots on the ground," vetting potential sites in Johannesburg and Durban to meet the specific cooling and power requirements of 2026-standard AI clusters. We de-risk the entry for international capital by handling pre-acquisition due diligence.
